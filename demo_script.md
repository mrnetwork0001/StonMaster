# StonMaster 3-Minute Demo Video Script

**Target Length:** ~3 minutes (approx. 450-500 words speaking)
**Tone & Pacing:** Conversational, enthusiastic, clear, and focused on the user experience.

---

### Segment 1: Introduction & Connect (0:00 - 0:30)
**Visual Action:** Start on the StonMaster Landing Page. Highlight the beautiful, neumorphic UI elements. Click "Connect Wallet" to show the seamless Privy / TON Connect modal.
**Script:**
> "Hey everyone, welcome to StonMaster—the ultimate premium utility hub built specifically for the TON blockchain. 
> Right now, the TON DeFi ecosystem is growing fast, but managing your assets, interacting with staking contracts, and finding the best swap routes can be fragmented and frustrating. 
> We built StonMaster to bring all these core primitives under one roof, wrapped in a blazing-fast, intuitive interface. Let me show you what it can do. First, let's connect our wallet..."

---

### Segment 2: Sweep Module - Decluttering the Wallet (0:30 - 1:15)
**Visual Action:** Once logged in, navigate to the dashboard or "Sweep" tab. Show the wallet overview. Point out how the interface smartly hides zero-balance tokens. Select a few small "dust" balances to sweep.
**Script:**
> "Once connected, you land on your unified dashboard. If you've been active on TON, you know how quickly your wallet accumulates 'dust' and empty jetton accounts that clutter up your portfolio.
> With our integrated Sweep Module, StonMaster automatically filters out those zero-balance accounts so you only see what matters. With just a couple of clicks, you can select those tiny token balances and sweep them, keeping your wallet perfectly clean and organized."

---

### Segment 3: Staking Module - Effortless Yield (1:15 - 2:00)
**Visual Action:** Switch over to the "Staking" tab. Show the clean layout highlighting APY rates. Type an amount of TON to stake, and bring up the seamless transaction prompt.
**Script:**
> "Next, let's talk about putting your assets to work. We've built a native integration with Tonstakers to provide a rock-solid staking experience directly inside the hub. 
> From a technical side, we've implemented extremely robust SDK initializations, meaning no more frustrating eager-loading errors—it simply works on the first try. You enter the amount of TON, review your projected yields, and with one click, your transaction is securely sent to the network."

---

### Segment 4: SocialSwap - The Standout feature (2:00 - 2:45)
**Visual Action:** Navigate to the "SocialSwap" section. Select two tokens to swap (e.g., TON for a popular memecoin). Show the UI quickly calculating the best route via Omniston. Click the "Share Route" button to demonstrate the custom link generation.
**Script:**
> "Finally, let me show you our standout feature: SocialSwap. This isn't just another standard swap interface. We leverage the Omniston API to instantly aggregate and fetch the absolute best trade routes across the network. 
> But we took it a step further to make DeFi social. If you find a highly profitable route or an arbitrage opportunity, StonMaster lets you instantly generate a custom, shareable link powered by Supabase. You can drop this link into your community or alpha chat, and anyone can execute that exact setup in a single click."

---

### Segment 5: Outro & Call to Action (2:45 - 3:00)
**Visual Action:** Return to the main dashboard to show the portfolio overview again. Fade out to the StonMaster logo with the website URL and social handles.
**Script:**
> "And that's a quick look at StonMaster. We've built an insanely fast, mobile-friendly hub that makes interacting with the TON blockchain feel like a premium Web2 experience. 
> Thank you for watching the demo. Check out our live link to experience StonMaster for yourself, and let’s elevate the TON DeFi ecosystem together!"
